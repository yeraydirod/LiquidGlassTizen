# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- **CLI** (`aerotizen`): TV discovery, pure-Python sdb client (no Tizen Studio), Samsung
  certificate creation, `.wgt` signing and install, app listing and debloat, remote keys.
- **Launcher**: 3-across app grid with official logos, inputs with device names, Fireplace,
  31 backgrounds (16 animated, adjustable speed), weather, date/time formats, reorder by
  holding OK, starts at power-on and reclaims the screen from Samsung Home.
- **Docs**: settings guide and DNS blocklists for ads and viewing-data tracking.
